Birendra Prasad

Owner, Birendra Shoe Shop

This shop began in 1976 on Beleghata Road with one small counter and a promise: sell only what I would put on my own family's feet. That promise has not changed in fifty years.

Many of my customers now send their children and grandchildren to buy shoes here. When a man tells me his father bought his first pair from this counter, that is the best thing anyone can say to me.

Come in, sit down, try the shoe, walk a little. If it does not feel right, we will find one that does.
